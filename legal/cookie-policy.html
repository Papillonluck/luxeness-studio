Cookie Policy

How we use cookies.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site recognise your device and remember preferences across visits.

How LUXENESS Studio uses cookies

This website is operated by Iconic CEO Limited, a company registered in England and Wales. We use cookies only where they support a clear function on this site.

We set one cookie ourselves. The third-party services listed below may set their own when they load:

Consent cookie. When you click Allow or Decline on the cookie banner, we set a single cookie (luxeness_cookie_consent) that records your choice so the banner doesn't reappear on every visit. This cookie expires after twelve months.

We do not use analytics cookies, advertising cookies, social media cookies or tracking pixels. We do not share data with third parties for marketing purposes. We do not profile visitors.

Third-party services

Three third-party services load as part of this website. When they load, your browser connects to their servers and may send technical information including your IP address and browser information.

Google Fonts. The typefaces used on this site are served by Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser requests those fonts from Google and Google receives your IP address as part of that request.

Typeform. The enquiry form is provided by Typeform. The form loads when you select a booking button, because you have asked to open it. If you select Allow on the cookie notice, it loads in advance so it opens instantly; if you select Decline, it loads only when you choose to book. If you open or complete a form, Typeform will also process the information you enter and may use cookies in connection with the form. Typeform’s own privacy policy governs its processing of that data.

Calendly. Call scheduling is handled by Calendly, which loads as part of the Typeform booking flow. Calendly loads only when you reach that step of the form. It may set its own cookies and processes the name, email address, timezone and chosen call time you enter. Calendly’s own privacy policy governs its processing of that data.

We do not receive analytics, profiles or marketing data from either provider, and we do not share your data with anyone for marketing purposes.

If this changes

If we introduce additional cookies in future, for example basic analytics to understand how the site is used, this policy will be updated and the cookie banner will give you the choice to accept or decline them before they are set.

Managing cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings, including blocking cookies entirely or deleting cookies already set. Most browsers allow you to manage this in their preferences or settings. Disabling cookies entirely may affect how some websites function, though LUXENESS Studio itself remains usable.

Your rights under UK GDPR

You have the right to be informed about the cookies we use, to refuse non-essential cookies without consequence, and to withdraw consent at any time by clearing cookies from your browser and reloading the page.
